Water for 4 astronauts in 30 sols

Kilograms (1 kg of water = 1 litre). Nothing is recycled: it all comes from Earth.

Maths: 4 people × 30 sols (30.8 Earth days) × NASA's daily numbers per person: drink 2.00 kg, water added to food 0.50 kg, extra drinking for exercise sweat 0.717 kg, total 3.217 kg. An early planetary base with a 1.08 kg shower uses 4.48 kg. Sources: NASA BVAD 2022 p.59 and Table 4-20; NASA ICES-2023-265 (water is "provided as a consumable and thus will not be recycled").
