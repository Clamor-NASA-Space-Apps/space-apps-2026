What 4 astronauts use in 30 sols

Kilograms, if nothing is recycled. That is NASA's plan for the first 30-sol stay on Mars: bring it all in tanks and packs.

Maths: 4 people × 30 sols (30.8 Earth days) × NASA's daily numbers. Water 3.217 kg (drink 2, food 0.5, sweat from exercise 0.7; washing not included). Packed food 2.39 kg, packaging included. Oxygen 0.84 kg. Sources: NASA BVAD 2022; NASA ICES-2023-265 (first Mars missions). Fact-check rows 112, 123, 132.
